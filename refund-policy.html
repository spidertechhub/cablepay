Strict No-Refund Policy

Please read carefully: All agency, referral, processing, and telephone concierge service fees charged by independent representatives are strictly non-refundable once service is rendered.

1. Nature of Service & Instant Execution

CablePay operates as an independent telephone concierge and billing referral platform connecting consumers with specialized independent agents. The fees paid for agent assistance, bill navigation, and processing time are earned immediately upon completion of the phone consultation.

2. Non-Refundable Fee Structure

Because representative time and technical payment routing occur in real time, all service fees are final and non-refundable. No full or partial refunds will be granted once your phone call has concluded.

3. Carrier Bill Payments vs. Service Fees

Direct funds transmitted to your official telecom/satellite carrier (e.g., SiriusXM, Xfinity, Spectrum, DirecTV, Frontier, Optimum, Verizon) are credited straight to your carrier account.
